Scene 02 · The thread

Look closer. That is the difference.

From a step away, good silk and great silk look alike. Under the loupe they do not: count the twist in the zari, the density of the weft, the way a motif ends without a knot.

Real zariSilver wire drawn to 0.08 mm, plated in 24 carat gold, then wound on silk.
Kadhua weaveEach motif is woven in separately by hand, so the back stays clean. No floats to cut.
38 hoursfor one border motif, at two weavers per loom.
