Independent type and brand studio in Basel since 2009. We design identities, typefaces and campaigns for public institutions and the companies that keep a city running.

What we do

A good identity is mostly restraint: one typeface used well, a grid that holds, a color people can name. We start there, draw what is missing, and stay until the system runs without us. Most of our clients have been with us for more than five years.
